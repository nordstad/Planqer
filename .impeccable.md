# Planqer design guidance

## Design Context

### Users

Woodworkers and fabricators planning real cuts in a workshop context. They need
to turn dimensions, stock, and CAD models into trustworthy cutting plans with
minimal input friction and no ambiguity about units or precision.

### Brand Personality

Practical, precise, and warm. Planqer should feel like a dependable workshop
tool: technically capable without looking clinical or demanding specialist
software knowledge.

### Aesthetic Direction

Workshop editorial. Preserve the restrained warm-neutral palette, orange signal
colour, Archivo typography, fine dividers, and compact tabular structure. Both
light and dark themes should remain supported. Avoid decorative dashboard
patterns, excessive cards, and consumer-app softness that obscures dense working
information.

### Design Principles

1. Make measurements, units, constraints, and consequences immediately clear.
2. Minimise clicks and repeated choices in frequent planning workflows.
3. Use spacing and alignment to make dense forms scannable without wasting room.
4. Prefer calm, workshop-appropriate precision over decorative effects.
5. Meet WCAG 2.2 AA for contrast, focus, keyboard interaction, and target sizing.
